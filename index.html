          What counts

          • A built project: a sensor, an app, a prototype, a model, a garden system
          • An invention or design, even if it is only on paper
          • A clearly explained idea for a climate problem in your community
          • Group projects: one teammate submits and lists the project

          What you will need

          • Your first name and school
          • A project title and a description of up to 80 words
          • Optional: a link to a photo of the project

          Privacy rules

          • We only show your first name, school, and country.
          • Your email stays private. We use it only to contact you about your entry.

          • Under 13? A parent or guardian must submit for you.

            About us

            Why NextGen Climate exists

            A call to action

            In the summer of 2025, co-founder Jiarui Li took part in Brown's Leadership Institute Summer Entrepreneurship Program. She noticed that many climate organizations focus on raising awareness but stop short of actionable solutions. In a world that advances through technology, young people can use that same innovation to solve problems. Back home, she shared the idea with two friends, and together they turned it into NextGen Climate.

            Our mission

            As infrastructure and innovation advance, the environmental consequences are too often overlooked. NextGen Climate empowers youth leaders to explore and create climate solutions through engineering, technology, and community action. We educate, inspire, and mobilize young people to become bold problem-solvers and strong voices for a sustainable future.
